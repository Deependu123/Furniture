The workshop

Slow work, made visible

Our furniture is not assembled from panels and glue alone. Frames are seasoned, joints are cut by hand where it matters, and every surface is sanded through progressive grits before finishing.

  1. 01

    Select & season

    We choose boards for grain direction, stability and colour continuity.

  2. 02

    Cut & join

    Mortise-and-tenon, dovetail and dowelled joints are selected by load and use.

  3. 03

    Finish by hand

    Natural oils and waxes protect the timber while keeping its texture open.

Materials

Wood with a record

We work with responsibly sourced hardwoods and finishes chosen for durability, repairability and a natural feel.

Teak

Dense, oil-rich and exceptionally stable. Ideal for dining tables and pieces exposed to daily use.

Signature

Sheesham

Rich Indian rosewood with pronounced grain. Excellent for beds, cabinets and statement storage.

Classic

Mango wood

Warm, sustainable and versatile. Used across sideboards, shelving and lighter occasional furniture.

Sustainable

Natural oil

A breathable finish that deepens the grain and can be locally repaired rather than replaced.
